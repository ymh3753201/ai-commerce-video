# Platform Safe Zones

Use this reference when placing subtitles, logo, price, offer, CTA, product close-ups, disclaimers, and lower-third text. Safe zones are planning guides; exact overlays vary by app version, device, locale, caption length, and ad format.

## General Rules

- Keep the product and face in the visual center unless the platform's UI forces a different composition.
- Keep CTA, price, discount, and disclaimer away from bottom overlays.
- Avoid putting important text on the far right of vertical social videos because profile, like, comment, share, shopping, and CTA elements often sit there.
- Put subtitles in short chunks; do not stack long captions over product details.
- If a platform supplies official safe-zone templates, use them before final publishing.

## Safe-Zone Profiles

| Profile | Use for | Planning rule |
|---|---|---|
| `center_80_percent` | Marketplace general fallback | Keep important text/logo/CTA inside the central 80% of the frame. |
| `tiktok_in_feed` | TikTok / TikTok Shop | Use vertical `9:16`; keep headline, price, logo, and CTA clear of bottom caption/CTA area and right-side action icons. Use official TikTok safe-zone templates when available. |
| `douyin_in_feed` | Douyin / Douyin Shop | Same vertical-first logic as TikTok, with extra care for shopping anchors, live-room badges, and bottom CTA overlays. |
| `xiaohongshu_note` | Xiaohongshu | Keep key copy in the middle; avoid exaggerated sticker stacks and bottom-heavy disclaimers. Make review/subtitle text readable without covering the product. |
| `amazon_desktop_mobile_video` | Amazon video ads / Sponsored Brands style video | `16:9`; avoid letterboxing, black frames, fake clickable buttons, and tiny disclaimers. Message should work muted. |
| `amazon_product_feature_video` | Amazon Sponsored Products Video | `16:9`; keep product large, feature text readable, and avoid talking-head or audio-dependent layout. |
| `youtube_universal_safe_zone` | YouTube / Shorts / Google Ads video | Keep logo, product, supers, CTA, and disclaimers inside YouTube's universal safe-zone overlays for horizontal, vertical, or square video. Apply ABCD structure. |
| `meta_reels` | Meta Reels / Instagram Reels | `9:16`; keep critical text away from top profile area, bottom CTA/caption area, and side action icons. Check Meta placement previews. |
| `product_page_safe_margins` | Shopify / independent product page | Leave clean margins for page chrome, captions, play controls, and responsive cropping; avoid text near edges. |

## Layout Checklist

Before image generation, include in the plan:

1. Aspect ratio and platform-safe layout.
2. Subtitle position and max line count.
3. Product hero area.
4. CTA area.
5. Disclaimer area when needed.
6. Which generated image will be checked for safe-zone compliance.

After image generation, ask the user to confirm:

1. Product is not hidden by captions.
2. Price/offer/CTA is readable.
3. Presenter face and product are not cropped.
4. Disclaimer is readable if present.
5. No platform UI area contains critical information.
